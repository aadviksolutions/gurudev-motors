Adapt useful content from the existing live homepage without copying every section or changing the approved rotating hero.

Recommended: reuse the existing homepage bands with simpler headings and short descriptions. An alternative is to add separate sections for every topic, but that creates a much longer mobile page. A gallery-led layout is another option, but puts browsing vehicles and contacting the showroom further down the page.

## Content And Layout

- Used bikes and exchange: make the existing exchange area explain both options, with separate links to used stock and exchange. Keep the payment-plan area alongside it on desktop and above it on phones.
- Workshop care: retain the service band, with "Bike service and repairs", a short description and the existing booking link.
- About us and showroom: combine them in the existing final visit band. Explain the five-brand offering in one short paragraph, retain address, hours, phone and directions, and link to About us and Showroom photos.
- Electric vehicles: keep the current category shortcut and stock filter. Do not add unverified electric brands, savings, subsidies or availability claims.
- Inside Gurudev Motors: show two existing showroom photos in a compact strip near the visit section, with a link to the gallery. Keep the full place visible and use descriptive captions. Show the same photos in the development gallery so the link has a useful destination.

## Boundaries And Verification

Frontend content and layout only. Reuse existing links, components and styles; no new backend calls, forms, stock claims or production changes. Check 320-430px phones and desktop for wrapping, section length and readable actions. Preserve the approved hero and its fallback.
